# TWO preview FAQ — the top 10 questions

Short answers to what newcomers actually ask. If yours isn't here, ask in
general or DM a moderator — there are no stupid questions in week one.

## Getting in

### 1. What is Together We Own?

A close-knit gaming clan, running since 1998, mostly evenings, 18+. We spent
most of our life private; now the lobby is open and you can just turn up.
Small enough that people notice when you come back.

### 2. How do I join?

Approve once with Discord on the join page and we'll add you to the server —
or use the Discord invite link instead. Then accept the rules on Discord's
membership screen: that's the gate, and it's how we know you're really in.

### 3. Do I need an invite, referral, or eligibility check?

No. The doors are open — no invite code, no referral, no waitlist. If you can
open the join page, you're eligible.

### 4. Is there an application, interview, or skill requirement?

No application, no interview, no tryout. Everyone starts as a Prospect: show
up a few times, play, become a Member. The ladder records trust and time, not
grind.

## Your first week

### 5. I joined but I can't post — what now?

You're at a locked door: Discord holds new members as pending until they
accept the rules on the membership screen. Accept them and you're in. If you
joined a while ago and never accepted, that 30-second step is the whole fix.

### 6. What should I do first?

Three things: pick your games (that grants you each game's role and opens its
channels), say hi in general, and come back once that week. Your first message
starts a friendly clock — we measure how fast a newcomer gets their first
human reply, so saying hi is genuinely contributing. Showing up twice is what
turns a join into a regular; ten minutes in voice counts the same as a message.

## Ranks and rewards

### 7. How do ranks, XP, and role rewards work?

Hanging out earns XP: messages earn 15 XP (at most once a minute), voice time
earns 5 XP per minute. Check yourself anytime with `/rank`, see the top ten
with `/leaderboard`. At certain levels the bot grants you a role reward
automatically — it never takes an earned reward away. Bots earn nothing, so
the ladder is humans only.

## Events

### 8. When do you actually play together?

**Sunday Squad, every Sunday at 8pm Eastern**, about an hour in the Lobby
voice room. It runs whether there's two of us or eight. Coming back once that
week — event or not — is what makes you a regular.

### 9. Do I need the game, a mic, or any experience?

No purchase, no skill, no sign-up, no need to say you're coming — just hop
into the voice room. The current game is Fall Guys, which is free on PC,
PlayStation, Xbox, Switch, and Android; if you haven't got it installed,
there's something we can play in the room itself. Check the event post for
that week's details.

## Privacy and conduct

### 10. What do you store about me, and what are the rules?

We store Discord user IDs, timestamps, and channel IDs — enough to count
joins and reporters honestly. We never store message content, email, location,
or voice audio. Private support tickets keep a staff-only transcript for 90
days. Ask anytime to be removed and we delete your rows.

The conduct version is one line: **be someone a nervous newcomer is glad to
meet.** No harassment, no spam or sales DMs, no raid behavior. Staff calls are
final in the moment; appeal afterwards by DMing a moderator, calmly, once.
